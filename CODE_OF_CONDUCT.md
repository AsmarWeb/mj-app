# Code of Conduct | قواعد السلوك

## Our commitment

murwaj welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. We aim to maintain a respectful, constructive and inclusive community, including Arabic and English speakers.

## Expected behavior

- Treat others with respect and assume good intent when reasonable.
- Give actionable feedback about ideas and code, not personal characteristics.
- Welcome questions, different viewpoints and first-time contributors.
- Respect privacy, intellectual property and other people's time.
- Accept corrections and help resolve disagreements calmly.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, sexualized content directed at participants, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, publishing private information without permission, spam and repeated disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions and other project-managed spaces, and when someone represents murwaj publicly.

## Reporting

Do not disclose sensitive conduct complaints in a public issue. Contact the maintainer privately using a private contact method listed on [AsmarWeb's profile](https://github.com/AsmarWeb). If no private method is listed, do not post the details publicly; wait for a private channel to be available.

Share only the information needed to assess the incident: what happened, relevant links, dates and the outcome you are seeking. Do not include unrelated personal information. Maintainers should limit access to reports to people handling the incident, but cannot promise absolute confidentiality.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may clarify expectations, remove content, issue warnings or temporarily or permanently restrict participation according to severity and repetition. People involved in a complaint should not decide its outcome. Requests to reconsider a decision can use the same private channel and should include relevant new information.

## بالعربية

نرحّب بالجميع ونطلب التعامل باحترام وتقديم نقد بنّاء. يُمنع التحرّش والتمييز والتهديد والإساءة ونشر المعلومات الخاصة. قد تُحذف المشاركات المخالفة أو تُقيّد المشاركة. تُرسل الشكاوى الحسّاسة عبر وسيلة تواصل خاصة يعلنها المسؤول، ولا تُنشر في Issues عامة.
